# Contributing

Thank you for helping maintain this evidence repository.

## Frozen corpus
The current systematic-review corpus is frozen at `S001–S145`. Existing IDs must not be renumbered or silently changed.

## Proposing a new study
Open an issue using the **Add Paper** template and provide the title, year, venue/status, DOI/URL, model/system, clinical domain/task, dataset/cohort, evaluation procedure, and coding-relevant details for fusion, retrieval, tools/agents, personalization, safety, and translation.

## Eligibility
Candidate studies should be original empirical studies in which a generative LLM, MLLM, or LLM-centered retrieval, reasoning, tool-use, or agentic framework contributes substantively to clinical inference, generation, or decision support.

Standalone encoder-only PLMs and conventional multimodal models without a substantive generative LLM component are outside the primary quantitative scope.

When multiple reports describe the same study, prefer the most complete peer-reviewed version.

## Pull requests
Do not edit the frozen workbook directly in a routine paper-addition PR. Proposed additions should be placed in a separate candidate file/issue until formally screened for the next review update.
